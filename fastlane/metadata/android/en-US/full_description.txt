<p><b>Hongeet</b> is a lightweight music player built for clean, fast playback. &#127911;</p><p>It supports <b>offline local audio playback</b> and optional streaming from third-party services such as <b>YouTube Music</b> and <b>JioSaavn</b> without any Ads. &#128640;</p><p><b>Important:</b> Streaming requires enabling one of the supported services. Without enabling a streaming source, Hongeet works as a local audio player only.</p><p><b>Why Hongeet?</b></p><ul><li>No paywall-first experience &#128184;</li><li>No forced account flow &#128275;</li><li>Clean and distraction-free interface &#129533;</li><li>Built for smooth playback and everyday use &#9889;</li></ul><p><b>Main Features</b></p><ul><li>Offline local audio playback</li><li>High-quality music streaming from supported services</li><li>Smart stream caching for faster repeat plays</li><li>Download songs for offline playback &#128229;</li><li>Background playback with lock screen and notification controls &#128276;</li><li>Queue management with loop modes (off / all / one) &#128257;</li><li>Recently played history &#128344;</li><li>Data Saver mode for lower bandwidth usage &#128246;</li><li>Customizable UI options (including Glass theme) &#127912;</li></ul><p><b>Privacy and Control</b></p><ul><li>No backend servers operated by Hongeet</li><li>Playback history and local data stay on your device</li><li>Streaming connects directly to the selected third-party service</li><li>Open source and transparent development &#128736;&#65039;</li></ul><p><b>Important Notice</b></p><p>Hongeet does not host or store copyrighted content.
