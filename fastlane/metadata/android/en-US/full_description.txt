Discovery</b> &#128270;</p><ul><li>Quick Picks for instant playback suggestions</li><li>Trending Charts &#128200;</li><li>Trending Albums &#128191;</li><li>Better scroll stability and smoother section rendering</li></ul><p><b>Playback Reliability</b> &#129504;</p><ul><li>Lazy artwork enhancement for chart/album song lists &#128444;&#65039;</li></ul><p><b>Download Experience</b> &#128229;</p><ul><li>Direct download flow for supported sources</li><li>Bounded parallel downloads for better stability on mobile networks</li></ul><p><b>Library Experience</b> &#127932;</p><ul><li>Downloaded songs screen with playback and delete actions</li><li>Local audio mode for device files</li><li>Playlist management with add/remove flows</li><li>Recently played section for fast backtracking</li><li>Favorite songs accessible from player and library</li></ul><p><b>User Experience Notes</b> &#129517;</p><ul><li>Controls are optimized for one-hand usage where possible</li><li>Performance-aware UI behavior based on app settings</li><li>Cleaner loading feedback during stream resolution</li></ul><p><b>Privacy &amp;
